Working Method

The website must separate workshop work, on-site work, domestic, commercial, and vehicle upholstery. Mixing everything onto one page would confuse visitors and dilute search relevance.

Domestic Work

Sofa recovering, dining chairs, armchairs, beds, headboards, cushions, ottomans, foam and frame replacement for homeowners.

Commercial Work

Restaurant seating, banquettes, booths, office chairs, reception units, hotel headboards, salons, clinics, contract upholstery.

Vehicle Upholstery

Car, taxi, and van seats, campervan cushions, motorcycle seats — durable repairs for daily use and business fleets.

Workshop Working

Full strip-down, foam work, frame repair, sewing, stapling, fitting, quality checking, and finishing at the workshop.

On-Site Working

Site visits, measuring, fitting, booth seating, wall panels, inspections, urgent repairs for items that cannot move.

Safety Standards

Fire-retardant materials, PPE, safe tool and adhesive use, careful handling, quality checks before delivery.

Questions, Answered

Why does Parvesh Upholstery Ltd need 58 pages?

Each page targets one specific service or town, matching exactly what a customer types into Google. One broad "services" page competes for everything and ranks for nothing; 58 focused pages each become an entry point for enquiries.

Why include both domestic and commercial sections?

Domestic work provides steady volume; commercial contracts (restaurants, hotels, offices) provide higher order values. Separate sections speak to each audience in its own language without either diluting the other.

Will the website be easy to update?

Yes. WordPress with Elementor means new photos, projects, testimonials, and text changes are handled through a visual dashboard — no developer needed for routine updates. Handover includes guidance for your team.

Why add workshop and on-site working pages?

They answer the two practical questions every customer has: "do you collect my furniture?" and "can you come to us?". Explaining both builds confidence and pre-qualifies enquiries before the first call.

Can customers send photos with quote requests?

Yes — the quote form includes image upload so customers photograph their furniture directly. Accurate visual information means faster, more accurate quotations.

Will the website work properly on mobile?

Every template is built mobile-first and verified across phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop during Week 4. Most upholstery searches happen on mobile, so this is tested before launch, not after.

What happens after the website launches?

You receive full handover guidance, then AJ Nodes presents the separate digital-marketing proposal covering SEO, Google Business Profile, social media, and local growth to turn the site into an active lead engine.
